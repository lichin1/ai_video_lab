### 七人發起・南邑成立（0:34.60，27.4 秒）
04. [0:38.60] 民國八十五年九月，七位鄉親發起慈善籌備會。  
    *In September 1996, seven neighbours formed a charity preparatory group.*
05. [0:46.13] 八十六年元旦，堅山南邑慈善會正式運作。  
    *On New Year's Day 1997, the Jianshan Nanyi Charity Association began work.*
06. [0:53.37] 首任會長李慶瑞，把自家住所借作會址。

### 遷址・募款・立案（1:02.03，27.7 秒）
07. [1:06.03] 會員很快破百，八十七年遷到林山巷新會址。  
    *Members soon passed 100, and in 1998 it moved to Linshan Lane.*
08. [1:13.03] 會館另外募款興建，從不動用慈善基金。  
    *The hall was built with separate donations, never touching charity funds.*
09. [1:20.00] 九十年正式立案，黃金城當選首屆理事長。  
    *Registered in 2001, it elected Huang Chin-cheng as first chairman.*

### 薪火相傳（1:29.70，36.1 秒）
10. [1:33.70] 九十二年成立顧問團，陪伴會務一路向前。  
    *In 2003 an advisory group was formed to support its work.*
11. [1:40.57] 陳國泰四度出任理事長，會員成長到九百多人。  
    *Chen Kuo-tai served four terms as chairman; members grew past 900.*
12. [1:48.63] 一百零一年，大家終於搬進自己的會館。  
    *In 2012, members finally moved into a hall of their own.*
13. [1:55.00] 洪丰裕、沈玉文到李國忠，一棒接一棒傳承。  
    *From Hung Feng-yu and Shen Yu-wen to Li Kuo-chung, the torch passed on.*

### 急難救助（2:05.80，38.3 秒）
14. [2:09.80] 急難救助是堅山的核心，理監事每月輪流訪視。  
    *Emergency relief is its core; board members take turns visiting each month.*
15. [2:18.00] 前理事長陳國泰與夫人吳惠豐，至今仍親自訪視。  
    *Former chairman Chen and his wife Wu Hui-feng still visit in person.*
16. [2:25.80] 一一二年，協助高雄、台南、屏東急難個案八十八件。  
    *In 2023, it helped 88 emergency cases in Kaohsiung, Tainan and Pingtung.*
17. [2:34.93] 也為兩戶清寒家庭，辦妥完整的喪葬事宜。  
    *It also arranged complete funerals for two families in need.*

### 觀音山捐血（2:44.07，16.4 秒）
18. [2:48.07] 同年兩場觀音山捐血，共募得七百六十三袋熱血。  
    *Two blood drives at Guanyin Mountain that year gathered 763 bags.*

### 清寒慰問・獎助學金（3:00.43，23.0 秒）
19. [3:04.43] 九月，在仁武慰問五區六百七十戶清寒家庭。  
    *In September, 670 low-income households in five districts received aid.*
20. [3:12.90] 十一月，九十四位清寒優秀學子領到獎助學金。  
    *In November, 94 bright students in need received scholarships.*
